Privacy Policy for Image Tool

Effective date: September 4, 2026

Image Tool ("the App") is developed by Patch Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Image Tool does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use its main image-processing features.

Images selected by you are processed for the functions you request, such as compression, resizing, conversion, cropping, and batch compression.

2. Image Files

Images selected for processing are used only to perform the requested operation. The App does not require you to upload your images to a Patch Studio server.

Processed files may be saved to your device's Downloads/Image Tools folder when you choose to save them.

3. Advertising

Image Tool displays advertisements using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google.

AdMob may collect and use information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, and information about ad interactions for advertising, fraud prevention, analytics, and related purposes, subject to Google's policies.

For more information about how Google handles information, please review Google's privacy information and advertising policies.

4. Local Storage

The App may access files on your device when you select images or other files for processing. Processed files can be stored locally on your device.

The App's History feature may display files that were created by Image Tool and saved in its designated Downloads folder.

5. Data Sharing

Patch Studio does not sell your personal information. Image files processed by Image Tool are not uploaded to Patch Studio servers as part of the normal image-processing functions.

Advertising-related information may be collected or processed by Google AdMob as described above.

6. Children's Privacy

Image Tool is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or Image Tool, you can contact Patch Studio through the support contact method provided in the App.
