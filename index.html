Privacy Policy for Image Tools

Effective date: September 6, 2026

Image Tools ("the App") is developed by Patch Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Image Tools does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use its main image-processing features.

Images and other files selected by you are processed locally on your device for the functions you request, including compression, resizing, conversion, cropping, and batch compression.

2. Image and File Processing

Images and files selected for processing are used to perform the operation requested by you. Image Tools does not require you to upload your images to Patch Studio servers.

Processed files may be saved locally on your device in the Downloads/Image Tools folder when you choose to save them.

3. Advertising

Image Tools displays advertisements using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google.

Google AdMob may collect and use information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, and information about interactions with advertisements for purposes such as advertising, fraud prevention, analytics, and related services, subject to Google's applicable policies.

For more information about Google's handling of information, please visit Google's Privacy Policy .

4. Local Storage and History

Image Tools may access files on your device when you select images or other supported files for processing.

Processed files can be stored locally on your device when you choose to save them.

The History feature may display files created by Image Tools and saved in its designated Downloads/Image Tools folder.

The App does not upload your processed images to Patch Studio servers as part of its normal image-processing functions.

5. Sharing Files

Image Tools may provide Android's standard sharing functionality when you choose to share a processed image or file.

When you use the sharing feature, the selected file is passed to the Android sharing system and the application or service you choose. The handling of the shared file is then subject to the privacy policy of the service you select.

6. Data Sharing

Patch Studio does not sell your personal information.

Image files processed by Image Tools are not uploaded to Patch Studio servers as part of the normal image-processing functions.

Advertising-related information may be collected or processed by Google AdMob as described in the Advertising section of this policy.

7. Children's Privacy

Image Tools is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or Image Tools, please contact Patch Studio through the support contact information provided for the App.
